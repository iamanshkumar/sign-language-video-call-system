## 1.5 Timestamp batches (a leakage risk metadata cannot resolve)

25,669 files are named `<word>_<date>_<time>_<n>.mp4`. They form 335 batches, each of one word; 332 batches are spread over train/val/test. 308 classes take more than half their clips from a single batch. Neighbouring words carry consecutive timestamps (`comb_…172637`, `cool_…172638`), so the timestamp is most likely the time a script processed the word, not a recording session - but if a batch is one signer or one recording, its val/test clips are not independent of its train clips. **Requires manual inspection.** The vocabularies cap any one batch at 50 % of a class.

| single_batch_dominated | > 50 % from one timestamp batch | possible same-recording leakage (§1.5) |

| deferred_pending_manual_inspection | config list | English homonym; one label may hold two signs |
